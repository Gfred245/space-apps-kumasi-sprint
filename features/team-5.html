HOW THE SCORE WORKS

Mars-likeness = 100 × (0.5 × coldness + 0.5 × dryness)
coldness = clamp((25 − t) / 45, 0, 1)
dryness = clamp(1 − r / 5, 0, 1)
t = temperature at 2 m (°C), r = rain (mm/day)

Source: NASA POWER · monthly averages Jan–Dec 2025 · built-in data
