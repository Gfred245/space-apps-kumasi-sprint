Base Camp Earth

Which place on Earth is most like Mars? This page shows real NASA weather data. Your team adds one feature to it.

Your job in 4 steps

  1. Ask an AI chat for one small feature. Copy the prompt from the green box below.
  2. Tap your team's button in "Team features" (for example Edit Team 3). It opens your file on GitHub.
  3. Delete the text between <!-- START --> and <!-- END -->, then paste the AI's code.
  4. Tap Commit changes, then Propose changes, then Create pull request. Tell the coordinator your team number. Your box below comes alive after they merge it.
Prompt to give the AI (change the part in brackets):
Write ONE self-contained HTML file (HTML, CSS and JavaScript in the same file, no libraries) for a small feature: [YOUR IDEA]. Under 80 lines, mobile friendly, with a title and large readable text.

Idea starters: a place picker (Atacama, Death Valley, Haughton) · a "Mars score" from temperature and rain · a countdown to a Mars launch window · a team colour theme · a Mars fact card with its source.
